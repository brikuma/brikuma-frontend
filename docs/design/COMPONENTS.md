Nontrivial behavior needs Testing Library interaction tests and browser coverage for real focus/route/renderer behavior. Do not unit-emulate async Server Components. Tests select accessible roles/names and verify independent expected outcomes, including protected errors, cleanup, no results and stale revisions.

The studio catalog exposes color before placement and reports width, depth and height in studs. Rotation swaps the horizontal footprint readout. Compact `Stud mount` / `Detail` badges derive from the public core connector query; they describe supported nominal sockets, not verified manufacturer parts. All catalog parts remain visible by default. A pending part remains a preview until a permitted canvas click or explicit Place action; its color and numeric destination remain editable. Cancel preserves model count, revision and history.

Placement has three explicit modes. **Snap to studs** uses the pointed-at top body plane and matches supported underside receiver sites to nominal stud centers. **Ground plane** ignores pointed-at geometry and requires the body to touch the ground. Both modes reject space occupied by the conservative bounds of catalog part bodies. **Free** permits composition without connector matching or that occupied-space guard; custom details start in this mode. The preview reports ground contact, aligned stud count, free placement or the rejection reason, with text in addition to color. Rejected placement never advances model count, revision or history. This is geometric feedback, not a general collision solver, physical connection verification or certification.

For standard integer footprints in assisted placement, pointer coordinates follow the support's translated, quarter-turned stud grid; odd/even footprint parity must not place a single stud between the support's studs. The ground grid uses cell centers. Fractional assisted footprints use a half-stud grid. Free pointer placement and numeric fields accept tenth-stud X/Z coordinates and twentieth-stud Y coordinates; numeric placement still respects the chosen placement mode. Direct transforms, rotation and duplication remain free-form core edits subject to protection, bounds and other model invariants. Front/Right views switch to Isometric for placement or horizontal Move; elevation buttons explain why they are unavailable while editing. Near-parallel rays are rejected before plane intersection to prevent distant clamped coordinates.

Inspect selected brick isolates a logical instance in the existing canvas, fits it closely and permits orbit underneath. The original model, count and history remain unchanged. Ground, grid, contact shadow and selection outline are omitted in this view so the underside is readable. Select, Move, an assembly view, new placement or explicit exit restores the full model and fits it again. Invalid/lost selection exits safely. Equivalent part dimensions remain available in the HTML inspector.

Assembly presentation consumes authored fixture metadata tied to a model baseline. **Roof access** moves the complete authored roof module while leaving the rest assembled; **Assembly** separates defined modules along their individual offsets. A 0–100% Separation slider changes only display offsets; dashed guides show original anchors and Reassemble restores the display. Part positions, count, BOM, revisions and history remain unchanged. New parts without authored assignments remain in place. Missing or incompatible metadata does not imply a generated assembly plan. The display is not a verified build order.

Dimension guides derive from declared part bounds, and selected parts have equivalent HTML readouts. They do not present uncalibrated centimeter values. Dimensions are hidden and their toggle disabled during assembly separation so presentation offsets cannot be mistaken for the model's dimensions. Camera fit uses visible part extents and waits for orbit controls to initialize. Studio quality adds screen-space occlusion; Balanced and Performance retain the same logical scene with less rendering work.
