# Frontend product context

ID: FE-PROD-001

Owner/scope: frontend repository

Status: Specified

Baseline: execution brief 1.1.0 / consolidated product v0.0.3, 2026-09-24.

The frontend translates the [global product principles](../../../docs/PROJECT_CONTEXT.md) into a navigable design studio with precise tools. A Mediterranean courtyard is the flagship sample, while a rover/mechanical study and creature/sculpture demonstrate broader intent. No subject category is required; free-form creation is a first-class path. Current visual decisions are owned by the [design system](../design/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md), not by the category of the sample model.

Primary users in this POC can explore curated examples, begin an empty model, inspect logical parts, make bounded local edits, compare a pre-authored sample variation and inspect coherent instruction/inventory views. The interface makes future AI, collaboration, marketplace and plans understandable without pretending their services exist.

The supplied D1/D2 product baseline is mapped in [baseline alignment](BASELINE_ALIGNMENT.md). Its wider ambition remains intact: an editable project with references, distinct visual/construction/technique/use choices, explicit verification coverage and coherent outputs. Current demo geometry, sample assistance and baseline instructions are bounded demonstrations of that direction, not substitutes for real catalog coverage or future assisted design.

Use a discreet persistent `Frontend preview` indicator. Contextual explanations belong beside unavailable actions; avoid warning-banner overload. Fictional people, reviews, listings, quantities such as owned stock, and any prices/allowances must be labeled examples.
